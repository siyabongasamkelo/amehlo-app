⚙️ Enforced Driving School Business Logic Rules

• The Absolute Financial Gate: If a student has lessonsAvailable === 0 or paymentStatus === "Pending", the dashboard hard-locks the interaction path. The student is badge-flagged as BLOCKED: NO CREDIT and cannot be inserted into active cars.
• The "Skip / No-Show" Routine: Clicking skip gracefully ejects a missing student from their slot, moves them to the back of the queue as a fallback backup standby, and slides the next waiting candidate up instantly to protect fleet runtime productivity.
• Fluid ETA Timelines: Replaced strict calendar blocks with fluid rolling timers (Position x 40 mins) labeled as approximation buffers to cushion against real-world traffic gridlocks.
• The R100 VIP Bypass: Upgrading a token to premium forces the resequencing engine to prioritize that card array positioning, instantly moving them ahead of standard slots to the next available position directly behind the active driver.
