AmehloApp is a high-velocity, queue-based management platform built specifically for driving schools to eliminate operational 
friction and maximize revenue without changing how they naturally operate. Moving away from rigid, traditional booking calendars, 
the system digitizes a real-world, first-come-first-served token system where students simply check into a live dashboard queue 
and wait for their token to be called. To unlock massive profitability, the platform features a premium priority-pricing engine 
allowing busy students to pay a convenience fee (e.g., R100) to reserve specific high-demand slots and instantly skip to the 
front of the line. Most importantly, AmehloApp integrates a silent, enterprise-grade AI facial recognition layer that cross-references
 a snapshot of the driver’s seat against the student’s master biometric profile at the start of every session—wiping out instructor 
 fraud, vehicle misuse, and "ghost lessons" to give owners absolute financial clarity and a bulletproof, 100% verified overview of 
 their daily business operations.
