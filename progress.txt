🛑 Locked-In Business & Operations Rules

1. The Strict Financial Gate: If a user's master profile has lessonsAvailable == 0 or paymentStatus == "Pending", the dashboard completely disables the check-in queue action button and throws a red "BLOCKED: NO CREDIT" warning badge. No profile, no queue.
2. The "Skip / No-Show" Mechanic: Clicking the "Skip / Standby" button on the active card immediately shifts a missing student from their active slot and sends them gracefully to the very back of the timeline row, sliding the next available driver forward so vehicles never idle.
3. Fluid Rolling ETA Duration: Replaced rigid static clock allocations with fluid estimations. Wait calculation scales flexibly: (Position - 1) × 40 mins, preventing psychological driver friction during real-world traffic gridlocks.
4. The VIP Priority Jumper: Paying the R100 convenience fee toggles the isPremium flag, instantly restructuring the queue indices to place that student ahead of all standard users directly behind the active driver car.
